# Groww video brand guidelines (editing + motion graphics)

Transcribed from the guideline pages the user shared on 22 Oct 2026 (originals in `source/`). Every video agent reads this file first. Where the source was unreadable or ambiguous it is marked **[confirm]**. The corporate brand sheet (PDF, colours + typography) arrived later the same day; see section 3 and the conflicts list.

Note: the example frames carry "Groww Edge" branding and are 16:9 with a seated, desk-based presenter. How each rule maps to 9:16 IPO Review Shorts with a standing green-screen presenter is listed in "Adapting to Shorts" at the end. **[confirm]**

## 1. Editing style: "One camera, constant motion"
A single locked-off A-roll made dynamic by punch-ins, dense inserts, and screen recordings that are art-directed, never raw.

### A-roll (talking head)
- Framing and set: single locked-off frontal camera, eye level, centred, seated at a desk.
- Two framings only: WIDE medium (head to desk, the base angle, about 60% of A-roll) and MCU punch-in at 115-130% crop (emphasis lines and number reveals).
- Switch wide <-> tight every 1-3 sentences, always on a jump cut.
- Punch IN on emphasis / claim / number; punch OUT on setup lines.
- Never hold the A-roll more than 15-20 s without a punch-in or insert.
- For side callouts, offset the presenter and darken/blur the empty side.

### Pace
- Hook minute: 20-28+ cuts. Sub-second B-roll, news montage, kinetic type every 1-3 s.
- Talking-head stretches: 14-21 cuts/min via punch-ins + inserts every 5-15 s.
- Screen demos: can drop to 0-5 cuts/min; motion comes from zooms, scrolls and highlight boxes, never a static hold.

### B-roll rules
- Official launch films and keynote clips for product news.
- News broadcasts with the original lower third / channel bug KEPT (it is proof).
- "Source: X" credit, small white sans, bottom-left or top-right, on every borrowed clip/number.
- AI-generated cinematic stock for lifestyle, metaphor and "old way vs new way" beats.
- Third-party YouTube clips in a bordered box with a source credit.
- No memes, no reaction GIFs, no cartoon stock.

### Transition vocabulary
- Dip to white / peach wash on the talking head (section change).
- Blur / fade-through between graphic states.
- Diagonal black panel wipe (also used for the end card).
- Brand-colour circle or band wipes when a sponsor/company is featured.
- Light-leak / grain flash, about 6-10 frames.
- Carousel slide: A-roll shrinks into a card and slides to the next story (news format).
- All moves: smooth ease-in-out with motion blur. NO bounce, NO shake.

### Sound design (inferred from captions + edit rhythm)
| Layer | Rule | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Music bed | Continuous, low, under VO the entire video; swell at section changes and the hook | Throughout |
| Whoosh | On every slide, carousel move, chapter interstitial, diagonal wipe | Transitions |
| Pop / click | On icon tiles, pills, cursor clicks, lower-third entry | Graphics |
| Typewriter | Soft ticks on type-on text and logo type-ons | Kinetic text |
| Riser / hit | Into the reveal: result first, verdict, round cards | Payoff beats |
| Original audio | News soundbites spliced raw into the hook montage | Hook |

### Do
- Front-load density: minute 1 is the busiest minute of the video.
- Change something visual every 2-4 s outside demos.
- Show the result before the process.
- Keep every number on screen with its source.
- Remove breaths and dead air.

### Don't
- No running subtitles on A-roll.
- No zoom-shake, glitch spam, bouncy easing, meme inserts.
- No raw full-screen screen recording without zoom / PiP / highlight.
- No persistent watermark or progress bar.
- No static holds over 5 s on any screen.

## 2. Motion graphics: "A restrained kit, one accent at a time"
A fixed CTA kit that never changes, plus per-video art direction. Everything is typed, slid or swept in with soft eases. Nothing bounces.

### Typography
- Main font: Inter Tight family. Impact / highlight font: IvyPresto Display family (italic serif for the emphasised words).
- Inter Tight / IvyPresto Display used as stand-ins and for highlights; ratio 70:30 max cap.
- Pattern seen throughout: sans line + one phrase in italic serif in an accent colour ("Why is IndiGo *hiding this fare?*").

### Motion rules (defaults; sync to VO first)
| Element | Entry | Hold / exit | Easing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kinetic text | Type on word by word, synced to VO; blur 8->0 px | Hold to end of phrase; cut or fade 150 ms | Ease-out (cubic) |
| Pills / icon tiles | Scale 90->100% + fade, 250-350 ms, stagger 60-80 ms | Group scales down + slides to make room | Ease-out, no overshoot |
| Article / doc cards | Slide in with 3D tilt ~8 deg, soft shadow, 400 ms | Slow push-in 2-3% while held | Ease-in-out |
| Highlighter | Wipe left->right 300-500 ms after the card settles | Stays | Linear-ish ease-out |
| Chapter interstitial | ~1 s total; light sweep diagonal across tile | Hard cut back to A-roll | Ease-in-out |
| Lower third | Slide up 12 px + fade, 300 ms | Hold 4-6 s; fade 200 ms | Ease-out |
| Screen zoom | Punch to 140-200% over 400-600 ms with motion blur | Track the cursor / scroll | Ease-in-out |
| Forbidden | Bounce, elastic, wiggle, shake, emoji pops, rainbow text | | |

### Templates
- **A. Kinetic text card:** one line, centred, light sans, ~3-4% of frame height; typed word by word in sync with the VO (not all at once); ONE keyword in accent colour (coral = money/warning, blue = emphasis); optional centred illustration or cut-out object below the line; ground: off-white #F8F7F5 + faint dot grid, or deep dark/indigo radial.
- **B. Sans + italic-serif line:** white regular sans with the emphasised phrase in italic serif; words blur/fade in one by one; smoky black ground with drifting fog for dark videos; doc/tweet cards can slide over the line with 3D tilt + shadow.
- **C. Text-in-frame (behind / beside presenter):** behind: giant heavy word, gradient fill, presenter matted on top (needs subject matte); beside: stacked 1-2 lines of condensed caps in the empty side, with a vignette on that side; one accent word (light blue / lime / orange); split lines either side of the head for quotes.
- **D. Chapter markers:** pick ONE per video and repeat it at every segment start. News: A-roll shrinks into lime-outline card, story pill, tick ruler, "02/12". Listicle: ~1 s black "#N" tile + diagonal blue light sweep. Explainer: numbered pill card returns with one more pill each time. Head-to-head: "Round N" poster card -> scoreboard update after verdict.
- **E. Article & tweet call-out:** recreate or screenshot the article as a clean card (thin lime border on black, or on paper); highlighter WIPES left->right across the key phrase (yellow-lime / coral ~60%); "Source: X" at top-right, small white sans; slow push-in; blurred edges for long pages.
- **F. Data & diagrams:** flat bars grow from the baseline; red dashed reference line; tables: winning column tinted, coral box on the row being discussed; diagrams: rounded nodes, thin connectors, icons pop in staggered, then the group scales and slides; numbered question pills on an arc that rotates like a wheel.
- **G. Hook mograph:** first 45 s fully designed, a cut every 1-3 s; huge type that crawls across the frame / opens a video window between words; tilted news/tweet collage sliding in with 3D tilt + shadow; outline type + solid white word-pills that pop with speech; pixelated/blurred preview of the payoff ("at the end I'll show you").
- **H. CTA kit (identical every video):** lower third: #141414 rounded box, serif copy, keyword in lime, "Link In Description", lime tag pill; community card: tilted 3D phone of the group, 3 bullets, "Click on the link below"; subscribe pill: white YouTube pill rises, cursor clicks "Subscribed" + bell; analytics card: "Watch time from subscribers: 70% not subscribed"; end card: diagonal black panel right, lime serif SUBSCRIBE, tagline, hand-drawn arrow, @handle -> QR card.

## Adapting to Shorts (proposed, not yet approved) **[confirm]**
- 16:9 desk framing -> 9:16: WIDE = waist-up, MCU = 115-130% punch on the face; same switching rules.
- Green-screen standing presenter instead of a desk set: backgrounds come from the chosen art-direction palette (paper + dot grid, or dark/indigo radial), not a busy scene.
- "No running subtitles on A-roll" conflicts with Shorts being watched muted. Options: follow it strictly (kinetic text only on key lines), or keep minimal subtitles only for Shorts.
- Kinetic text (A/B) replaces captions on key lines: typed word by word, one accent word.

## Colour meaning (user rule, 22 Oct 2026; applies to every video)
| Colour | Means | Use for | Never use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green #00D09C | Profit / positive | Gains, growth, oversubscription, "above 1x" | Neutral decoration where it could read as "good" |
| Red #FF5757 | Loss / caution | Losses, declines, risks, "below 1x", warnings | Decoration, or anything neutral |
| Yellow #F7CC69 | Disclaimer / note | Disclaimers, "important" notes, footnotes, things still pending | Data values |
| Blue #5367FC | Neutral emphasis | Structure, labels, titles, neutral numbers (price band, issue size, dates) | Profit or loss signals |
Exact red and yellow hexes follow the working defaults in section 4 until confirmed.

## Logo (user rule, 22 Oct 2026; applies to every video)
- The Groww logo sits **top-right on every video**, for the whole video. This is a deliberate user choice and overrides the video guide's "no persistent watermark" line for the logo.
- Files (extracted from the corporate PDF, transparent PNG, 1395x380): `logo/groww_logo_dark_text.png` (light backgrounds), `logo/groww_logo_white_text.png` (dark backgrounds / over footage), `logo/groww_icon.png` (icon only). Never redraw or retype the logo; never recolour the icon.
- Placement on 1080x1920: about 230 px wide, right edge 50 px from the frame edge, top about 80 px, on the same line as any top-left topic tag. Pick the dark-text or white-text file per shot so it always reads.
- Logo clear zone (user rule, 29 Oct 2026): nothing may sit around the Groww logo, not even something in the footage (wall stickers, switches, frames, text in the background). Keep the logo's whole area plus about 60 px around it (canvas x 740-1080, y 0-360) empty: paint marks out of the footage (`key.py --plain --clean X,Y,W,H`), reframe, or ask the user.
- Layout consequence: the top band (y 0-220) is reserved for the header row (tag left, logo right). Cards, end card and full-screen titles start below y 220 so nothing collides with the logo.
